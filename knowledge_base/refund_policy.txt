Refund and Return Policy

1. Return Window:
   - Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
   - Items must be unused, in original packaging, with intact tags and seal.

2. Non-Returnable Items:
   - Software licenses, digital downloads, hygiene items, and customized products are non-returnable unless damaged upon delivery.

3. Refund Processing Timelines:
   - Credit / Debit Cards: 5 to 7 business days after return verification.
   - UPI / Net Banking: 2 to 4 business days after return verification.
   - Cash on Delivery (COD): Refunded to verified bank account / UPI ID within 3-5 days after account details are provided.

4. Return Shipping Fee:
   - Free pick-up for defective, damaged, or incorrect items.
   - A nominal return fee of Rs. 100 applies for standard preference returns.

5. How to Initiate a Return:
   - Request a return via customer support or account portal under 'My Orders'.
