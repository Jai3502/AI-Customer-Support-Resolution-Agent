Warranty and Repair Policy

1. Standard Warranty Coverage:
   - All electronic products carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of invoice.
   - Warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware failures, and component malfunctions under normal usage.

2. Warranty Exclusions:
   - Physical damage, liquid spills, unauthorized repairs, or burn marks are excluded from warranty coverage.

3. Claim Process:
   - Provide invoice copy and product serial number.
   - Support generates a pickup label for diagnostic evaluation at an authorized service center.
   - Repair or replacement is completed within 7 to 10 business days.
