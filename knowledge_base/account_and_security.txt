Account & Security Policy

1. Account Security:
   - Always keep your password confidential and enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) in settings.
   - We will never ask for your account password or OTP via call, SMS, or chat.

2. Suspicious Activity & Fraud:
   - If you notice unauthorized orders or unexpected password reset emails, report them immediately.
   - Fraudulent transactions or unauthorized access will be escalated to our Security & Fraud team for account protection.
