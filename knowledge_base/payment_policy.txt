Payment Policy and Issue Resolution

1. Accepted Payment Methods:
   - Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Maestro)
   - UPI Payment (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
   - Net Banking across 50+ major banks
   - EMI Options on select credit cards
   - Cash on Delivery (COD) up to Rs. 10,000

2. Failed Transactions & Double Charges:
   - If payment is deducted but order fails, amount is auto-refunded by bank within 2-5 business days.
   - For duplicate payments on the same order, report to support for priority verification and immediate reversal.

3. Invoices & Receipts:
   - GST invoices can be downloaded directly from the order history page.
