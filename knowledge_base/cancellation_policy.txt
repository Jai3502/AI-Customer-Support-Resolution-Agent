Cancellation Policy

1. Pre-Dispatch Cancellation:
   - Orders can be cancelled instantly before dispatch via the app/website or through our support assistant.
   - Full 100% refund is initiated automatically upon cancellation.

2. Post-Dispatch Cancellation:
   - If the item has already been shipped, it cannot be cancelled in-transit.
   - Customers may refuse delivery at the doorstep or accept and initiate a 30-day return.

3. Automated Cancellation:
   - Orders may be cancelled by the system due to stock unavailability or address verification issues.
   - Full refund is processed immediately if pre-paid.
